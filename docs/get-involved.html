Before you go

A few habits that make any volunteer day go smoothly.

  • Confirm your sign-up with the organizer, and check their page for any last-minute changes.
  • Wear closed-toe shoes and layers, and bring water, sunscreen and a hat.
  • Look at the forecast. Events can change or close for weather.
  • Ask about age limits, group sizes and what tools or gloves are provided.
  • Arrive a few minutes early so the organizers can give a short safety briefing.
