Spot a Need

See a place that needs help? Tell us.

Take a few photos and tell us what you see. After we review it, your report goes on the Needs Board, where conservation groups can offer to organize a cleanup or restoration day there.

Spot a Need is not switched on yet. Please check back soon.

Stay safe, and keep it respectful

  • Do not touch needles, chemicals, broken glass or dead animals. Take a photo from a distance and say what you saw.
  • Stay on public land and open trails. Do not enter private or closed areas.
  • In an emergency call 911. For hazards such as sharps or dumped chemicals, also tell the park or your city or county.
  • Please keep people, faces and license plates out of your photos.
