About

Shoremark helps neighbors find ways to help

A free, independent project that gathers volunteer opportunities from local community groups into one easy place to search.

Why Shoremark exists

Great habitat work is happening every week around the Bay: beach cleanups, native plantings, marsh restoration, invasive plant pulls. But the listings are scattered across calendars, newsletters and social posts. If you only have a free Saturday morning, finding the right one can take longer than the volunteering itself. Shoremark puts them on one map and organizes them by habitat, so you can spend less time searching and more time showing up.

The name comes from the shore, where land, water and wild places meet, and from marking those places on a map.

How the listings work

We don't run these events and we don't take sign-ups. We make them easier to find.

Collected from public calendars

Events are gathered automatically from each group's own public event pages and refreshed on a regular schedule.

Linked to the source

Every event links to the organizer's own sign-up page, so you always register with the group you'll be helping.

Described with tags

Short tags such as outdoors, very active or beach are generated automatically from each event's title and location. Treat them as a guide.

How Shoremark relates to Civic Snap

Shoremark grew out of Civic Snap, a Bay Area local volunteering site by the same maker. Shoremark keeps the same approach, reading each group's own public calendar and linking back to its sign-up page, but focuses only on the environment. Civic Snap continues as the general-purpose volunteering site.
